Critical Station Exits Cheat Sheet 改札口案内

Shibuya
Hachiko Exit (ハチ公改札)

Direct access to the famous Scramble Crossing, Hachiko statue, and SHIBUYA 109.

Shinjuku
East Exit vs West Exit (東口 / 西口)

East Exit for Kabukicho & Isetan shopping; West Exit for Tokyo Metropolitan Gov. Towers.

Tokyo
Marunouchi vs Yaesu (丸の内 / 八重洲)

Marunouchi for Imperial Palace; Yaesu for Shinkansen bullet train & airport buses.
